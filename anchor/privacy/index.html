⚓ Anchor: ADHD Companion

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 7, 2025  ·  WorkStream Apps LLC

🔒 100% On-Device — Your data never leaves your phone
Anchor is built on a simple principle: your personal information belongs to you, stays on your device, and is never sent anywhere. We do not collect, store, or sell your data. This is not a policy full of exceptions — it is the entire point of how Anchor works.

What data Anchor stores

All data is stored locally on your device using SQLite. Nothing is ever transmitted to our servers or any third party. This includes:

  • Your routine names, icons, and schedules
  • Your daily check-off history and chain progress
  • Your capacity check-ins (how you felt each day)
  • AI-generated insights derived from your usage patterns
  • Your app preferences and settings

⚡ If you delete the app, all your data is deleted with it. We have no copy. No backup. No account to deactivate.

On-device AI

Anchor uses Apple Foundation Models (Apple Intelligence) to generate personalized companion messages. This AI runs entirely on your device — your data is never sent to Anthropic, OpenAI, or any other AI provider. Apple Intelligence requires iOS 26 or later and is only available on supported devices.

On devices without Apple Intelligence, Anchor uses pre-written static messages that are equally warm — just not personalized to your patterns.

Purchases (RevenueCat)

If you purchase Anchor Pro (lifetime or monthly), your purchase is processed through Apple's App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives only the information necessary to verify your purchase — no personal health data, no routine data, no check-off history.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Anchor schedules local notifications on your device. These are processed entirely on-device by iOS — no notification data is sent to our servers.

Analytics

We collect no analytics. There are no third-party analytics SDKs, no crash reporters that send personal data, no user tracking, and no advertising identifiers used in Anchor.

Children

Anchor is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Because all data stays on-device, there is no server-side collection regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will post the revised version here with a new effective date. Because we collect no personal data, any changes will relate only to how we describe our practices — not to new data collection.
