The Framework

The 5-Level AI Maturity Model

Most teams start at Level 0. Our consulting work moves you up the ladder systematically — each level builds on the last and delivers real outcomes before advancing.

0
Chat

Q&A and Conversation

Ask questions, get answers. Most teams start here — useful, but not yet leveraging context or tools. Where is your team today?

1
Build

Finished Deliverables From Your Files & Systems

Claude reads your deal folder and drafts the investment memo. Reads your pipeline and surfaces at-risk deals. Reads meeting notes and writes the follow-up. This is the Level 1 win we target in training.

2
Skill

Repeatable Workflows Encoded as Skills

The best-performer's process becomes one reusable skill. /weekly-pipeline-report runs the same analysis every time, in your format, in 90 seconds.

3
Automate

Skills That Run Without Being Asked

Morning briefing fires at 7:30am — calendar, pipeline, Slack digest — all in one page. The team starts the day already briefed. Work that was never staffed now happens automatically.

4
Scale

Department-Wide Plugins, Admin-Provisioned

IT deploys a Legal plugin to every attorney. A Sales plugin to every rep. Org-wide AI infrastructure, not individual power users.
