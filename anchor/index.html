Features

Built specifically for ADHD brains

Every design decision starts with one question: does this reduce friction or add it?

The Chain Never Breaks

A missed day adds a softer neutral link — the chain just keeps growing. Never red, never zero, never a failure state. One routine on a hard day counts more than five on an easy one.

Attach to Moments You Already Have

Pick a trigger — after coffee, before bed, after lunch — and your routine anchors to it. It stops being something to remember and starts just happening. This is why routines actually stick.

Swipe to Complete

Swipe the whole card to check off a routine — no hunting for a small button. Designed for variable attention and motor control. One satisfying motion, done.

Daily Capacity Check-in

Good day, okay day, or heavy day — tell Anchor once and it adjusts everything. Hard scenarios are bypassed. Encouragement matches your energy, not a template.

Weekly Reflection Letter

Every week, a short personal letter reads your actual week back to you — with kindness. Not a stats report. A voice that noticed what you kept going, even on the hard days.

AI That Learns You

Apple Intelligence runs entirely on your device. By week two, Anchor knows which days are harder, which routines stick, and what kind of day you're having — before you say anything.

How triggers work: you pick the moment, Anchor attaches to it

When you add a routine, you choose a trigger — an existing moment in your day. Your routine becomes part of something you already do, not a new thing to remember. Behavioral science calls this habit stacking. We just made it two taps.
