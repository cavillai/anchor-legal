Features

Built specifically for ADHD brains

Every design decision starts with one question: does this reduce friction or add it?

🔗
The Chain Never Breaks

A missed day adds a softer neutral link — the chain just keeps growing. Never red, never zero, never a failure state. Progress is cumulative, always.

🧠
AI That Learns You

Apple Intelligence runs entirely on your device. By week two, Anchor knows which days are harder, which routines stick, and what kind of day you're having — before you say anything.

👆
Swipe to Complete

Swipe the whole card to check off a routine — no hunting for a small button. Designed for variable attention and motor control. One satisfying motion, done.

🌡️
Daily Capacity Check-in

Good day, okay day, or heavy day — tell Anchor once and it adjusts everything. Hard scenarios are bypassed. Encouragement matches your energy, not a template.

✉️
Weekly Reflection Letter

Every week, a short personal letter reads your actual week back to you — with kindness. Not a stats report. A voice that stayed here while you were gone.

🔕
Gentle Reminders Only

Local notifications, scheduled by you, controlled by you. No marketing pings, no streaks-at-risk pressure, no anxiety-inducing alerts. You're in charge.

Your data lives on your phone.
Full stop.

No account. No server. No analytics. Everything Anchor knows about you stays on your device — and disappears when you delete the app. This is not a policy full of exceptions. It's how the app works.

📴
Fully Offline

Works with no internet connection. Always.

🚫
No Account

Nothing to sign up for. Nothing to deactivate.

🧠
On-Device AI

Apple Intelligence runs locally. Your data never reaches any AI provider.

📊
No Analytics

No trackers, no SDKs, no user profiling. Zero.
