Common Questions

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?

Open Anchor, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases." Your purchase is linked to your Apple ID and will restore automatically.

Is my data backed up?

Anchor stores everything on-device. Your data is included in your iPhone's iCloud backup automatically if you have iCloud Backup enabled in Settings.

How do I cancel my monthly subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions on your iPhone to cancel anytime.

Why isn't the AI companion showing on my device?

The AI companion requires Apple Intelligence, available on iPhone 16 or iPhone 15 Pro and later running iOS 26. The core chain and check-offs work on all supported devices.
